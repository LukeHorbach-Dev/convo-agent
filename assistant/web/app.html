Your daily routine

Evening. Request your download in Instagram (Accounts Center, Your information and permissions, Download your information: Messages and Followers and following, JSON, All time). When the email comes, download the file into Downloads, then press Load from Downloads. The app reads it and writes replies by itself.

Morning. Open Replies and Hellos, change anything you want, approve, then press Send everything I approved. It sends one at a time at a steady pace and you can carry on using your laptop.

When something goes wrong

The app sorts every problem into one of four kinds and tells you on the Today page:

Try again later: Instagram didn't load. It waits 10 minutes and carries on.

Skip this person: their account is private, gone, or doesn't take messages. Everyone else carries on.

Take a break: Instagram showed "try again later". It stops for 6 hours, then carries on by itself.

Stop and tell you: Instagram wants you to sign in again, the AI key or credit needs looking at, or Claude couldn't finish three times in a row. You get a Mac notification. Run bash check.sh and send Claude the check.txt.

How sending works

For each message, Claude looks at the Instagram page and clicks through to the chat, like you would. It checks it's the right person, that a hello goes into an empty chat, and that they haven't written again since your download.

Claude can't type. The app types the exact message you approved, checks it letter for letter in the message box, then presses Enter and checks it appeared in the chat.
