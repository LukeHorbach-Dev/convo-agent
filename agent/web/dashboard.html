How it works

Hellos. The app reads the people you follow. Anyone you haven't talked to yet gets one hello from your own list of hellos. It skips anyone you already have a chat with, anyone on your never list, and anyone it thinks is under 19.

Replies. When someone writes to you, the app reads the chat and writes a reply the way you write (if AI replies are on in Settings). Without AI it leaves the box empty for you.

Nothing goes out without you. Every message waits under "To check" until you press Send. At most 25 a day.

If it gets stuck

Instagram changes its website every so often. When the app can't find something, it stops that one person and tells you here, and nothing gets sent by mistake.

To check everything at once: press Control + C in Terminal, then type bash check.sh. It tries every step without sending anything or unfollowing anyone, then opens a folder. Send Claude the file check.txt.
