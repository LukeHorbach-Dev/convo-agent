CONVO __VERSION__ - your Instagram assistant and personal brand command centre

INSTALL (once)
1. Drag Convo into your Applications folder (choose Replace if it asks).
2. Open Terminal, paste this line and press Enter (it tells your Mac you trust this app):

     xattr -cr /Applications/Convo.app

3. Double-click Convo in Applications.
   The first time, a Terminal window sets it up (about 3 to 5 minutes), then Convo opens by itself.
   Leave that window until it says "All set".

If your Mac says Convo "can't be opened": open System Settings, Privacy & Security, scroll down,
and press "Open Anyway" next to Convo. Then double-click Convo again.

EVERY DAY
Just open Convo from Applications or the Dock. It keeps working in the background when you close its
window. To stop it completely, use "Quit Convo" in Account Settings.

UPDATES
You don't need to download Convo again. When there's a new version, Convo says "Convo x.y.z is ready".
Press Update now: it installs, backs up your data and restarts by itself.

EASIEST INSTALL (instead of steps 1 to 3): paste this into Terminal and press Enter:
     curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LukeHorbach-Dev/convo-agent/main/install.sh | bash

Your data, Instagram sign-in and settings live in ~/Library/Application Support/Convo and are always kept.
