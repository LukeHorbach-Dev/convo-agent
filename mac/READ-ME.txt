CONVO __VERSION__ - your Instagram assistant

INSTALL (once)
1. Drag Convo into your Applications folder.
2. Open Terminal, paste this line and press Enter (it tells your Mac you trust this app):

     xattr -cr /Applications/Convo.app

3. Double-click Convo in Applications.
   The first time, a Terminal window sets it up (about 3 to 5 minutes), then Convo opens by itself.
   Leave that window until it says "All set".

If your Mac says Convo "can't be opened": open System Settings, Privacy & Security, scroll down,
and press "Open Anyway" next to Convo. Then double-click Convo again.

EVERY DAY
Just open Convo from Applications or the Dock. It keeps working in the background when you close its
window. To stop it completely, use "Quit Convo" in its Settings page.

UPDATING
Download the new Convo, drag it into Applications and choose Replace. Your data, sign-in and settings
are kept (they live in ~/Library/Application Support/Convo).

Your old convo-agent folder in Downloads is copied across the first time (and left as it is).
