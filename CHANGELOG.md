# What's new in Convo

Each section is shown once in Convo ("What's new") after it updates to that version.

## 5.5.0-preview.1

Stage 6: growth, measured honestly.

- **Weekly Missions** with XP, levels, streaks and badges, filled in by themselves from real activity: real
  conversations (you both wrote), replying to everyone waiting, posting, planning, saving ideas, checking in with
  people you'd click with. Add your own too. There are no "send more messages" missions.
- **Growth Goals**: followers on any account, YouTube views, posts or conversations a week, by a date. Convo says
  on track or behind only once there's enough to judge.
- **Organic Independence Score**: from the parts that can really be measured (found by the platform, real
  conversations, genuine comments, posting consistently), with a suggestion on the launch network. No score until
  at least two parts are measured.
- **Launch mode** on the Launch Network: five steps per account, type in an account's numbers to build its
  history, and plan a shout-out from your main account in one click.
- **Collaborations**: track ideas with people from idea to done.
- **Engagement Assistant**: new Instagram and YouTube comments in one list, replies written in your voice for you
  to check. Instagram replies post only when you press Send; YouTube opens the comment for you.

## 5.4.0-preview.1

Stage 5: Instagram and TikTok.

- **Connect Instagram** (needs a free Creator account) and **TikTok**, each with your own free developer app. The
  steps are in Integrations, Set up. Both send you back to Convo through a small return page on your GitHub project
  (one switch in GitHub, Pages, also in the steps).
- **Analytics** shows each one's real numbers: followers, your latest posts with views, likes, comments and shares,
  and Instagram reach by day.
- **Publish from the Content Studio**: a Reel straight to Instagram (Convo asks first, as it's public), a video to
  your TikTok inbox as a draft that you finish in the TikTok app, or to YouTube as private.
- Hello Message keeps its careful browser method: the official Instagram API doesn't allow starting DMs.

## 5.3.0-preview.1

Stage 4: YouTube.

- **Connect your YouTube channel** (Integrations, YouTube API, Set up): six short steps make your own free Google
  app, then Connect signs in on Google's own page. Your app details and keys stay on this Mac.
- **Analytics** now shows YouTube's real numbers: subscribers, views and watch time by day for 28 days, where views
  came from, and your latest videos with views, likes and comments. Updated every 6 hours (or Update now).
- **Upload from the Content Studio**: a video post with YouTube chosen gets "Upload to YouTube (private)", with its
  title, description and tags. You make it public in YouTube Studio when you're happy.
- The AI Strategist uses your YouTube numbers too.

## 5.2.0-preview.1

Stage 3: your content, planned and written, and a strategist that works from real numbers.

- **Content Studio**: start an idea, video, short, photo or carousel. Drop the file in (it stays on your Mac), write
  your notes, choose Instagram, TikTok and/or YouTube, and press **Write with AI** for a caption, title and hashtags
  for each one, from your notes and your About me only (about 1 to 2 cents). A phone preview shows how it looks.
- **Content Library**: every idea, draft and post in one place, with search and filters.
- **Publishing Calendar**: month and week views. Drag a post to another day, or drag a ready post onto a day.
- **Scheduled Posts**: when a post's time comes, Convo tells you, with the words ready to copy and the file one
  click away (Show in Finder), then you mark it posted. Once your platforms are connected (stages 4 and 5) it can
  post for you.
- **AI Strategist**: answers from your real numbers (messages, hellos and replies, followers, follow-ups, your
  content plan), shows those numbers beside the chat, and never makes up a statistic. It can save post ideas to
  your library when you ask.
- The Dashboard's Upcoming list now includes your scheduled posts.

## 5.1.0-preview.1

Stage 2: everyday work in the new design, Convo on your phone, and follow-ups.

- **Review hellos and replies here**: Hello Message, Review hellos (or Review replies). Search a name, filter to
  the people you'd click with or the ones that need a look, edit, approve or skip one by one, or tick several at
  once. "Approve all ready" asks first (type APPROVE for big batches) and every action has **Undo**.
- **Phone access** (Settings, Phone Access): use Convo on your phone at home or away through Tailscale, a free
  private network between your own devices. Scan the code and it opens signed in. Off until you turn it on.
- **Follow-ups**: who's been waiting on you for 2+ days, hellos with no answer after a week, and reminders you set
  ("Remind me about them" on anyone in Contacts). Convo tells you on the day. Later and Done hide them, with Undo.
  People Convo mustn't contact (under 19, asking for money, your never list, chats you skipped) never show up.
- Nothing here sends anything. Messages still go only when you approve them and sending runs.

## 5.0.0

Convo 5, checked on your Mac.

- **The Command Centre** is your home screen: followers, conversations, hellos and what to do next, from your
  real Instagram data. The classic screens are one click away (bottom of the sidebar).
- **Always clear what's running**: the top bar says what Convo is doing, and Stop now changes straight away to
  "Stopped by you" with Start again.
- **Updates from GitHub**: Convo tells you when there's a new version. Click Update now; it backs up your data
  and restarts by itself, and "Go back" undoes it.
- **One-line install**, a **new logo**, its **own Mac window**, and it **repairs itself** if it can't start.
- Your 1,886 hellos are exactly as they were. Nothing is sent without your approval.

## 5.0.0-preview.5

- **Always clear whether anything is running.** The top bar shows what Convo is doing right now: "Nothing
  running", "Sending to @someone", "Writing replies 3 of 10", "Quiet hours", or "Stopped by you". Click it for
  the details.
- **Stop now changes straight away.** It shows "Stopping…", then "Stopped by you" with a **Start again** button,
  which asks before anything sends.
- On a phone, a slim bar under the top bar shows the same whenever something is running or stopped.
- The Hello Message page's buttons follow the same live state.

## 5.0.0-preview.4

- Fixed: Convo's window asking for a password. That happened when an older Convo was still running in the
  background with a different password. Opening Convo now replaces it by itself.
- If a window ever loses its connection, it says to open Convo again from Applications.

## 5.0.0-preview.3

- If Convo can't start, it now repairs itself once (it sets its parts up again; your data isn't touched), and if
  that doesn't help, the message box says what the problem is.
- Setup checks Convo starts the same way the Mac opens it from Applications, so a problem shows up straight away.

## 5.0.0-preview.2

- **A new logo**: a speech bubble shaped like a C, in Convo's violet and blue, on the Dock, in the window and on
  the classic screens.
- **One-line install**: paste one line into Terminal and Convo downloads, installs, sets itself up and opens.
- **Updates without a key** once the GitHub project is public (the key box only appears if it's needed).

## 5.0.0-preview.1

A preview of Convo 5: the new Command Centre, a proper Mac window, and updates straight from GitHub.

- **The Command Centre**: a new home screen with your followers, conversations, hellos and what to do next, built from
  your real Instagram data. The classic screens are one click away ("Classic screens" in the sidebar).
- **A real Mac window**: Convo opens in its own window, with its name and icon in the Dock.
- **Updates from GitHub**: when there's a new version, Convo says "Convo x.y.z is ready". Press Update now and it
  installs and restarts by itself. Your hellos, people, settings and Instagram sign-in stay as they are. It makes a backup first,
  and "Undo the last update" (on the Updates page) goes back to the version before.
- **Your hello queue is untouched**: nothing is sent, deleted or changed without your approval.
- Small things: page titles, a version line in the sidebar, an Updates page, and if one page ever hits a problem it
  says so on that page instead of the window going blank.
